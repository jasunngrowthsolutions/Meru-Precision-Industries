— 01 · About Us

Forty-three years of grinding, hardening and toolroom craftsmanship in Bengaluru.

Meru Precision Industries was incepted in 1983 in Rajajinagar, Bengaluru. Early work focused on fabricating ball races for two- and three-wheelers — a discipline that instilled mastery over heat treatment, cylindrical grinding, and micron-level bearing surfaces.

In the late 1980s and early 1990s, that foundation expanded into special tools, jigs and fixtures for broader industrial and automotive clients. Over the following decades the company diversified into full-spectrum metrology gauges, linear motion components, electronic hardware, and purpose-built special-purpose machines — always keeping the same discipline: drawing or sample in, conforming part out.

Today, Meru Precision operates as a build-to-print manufacturer and reverse-engineering partner, serving Tier‑1 automotive, aerospace, defense, hydraulics, electronics, and heavy engineering OEMs — with a working model that starts from your formal print, your worn sample, or an oral functional specification.

Build-to-print discipline

Tight adherence to your 2D/3D print, material spec, and acceptance criteria — with drawing sign-off before a single cut is made.

Sample-first workflow

Flexible consignment sizes

Batch quantities scaled to your schedule — from single-piece spares to repeating production runs.

Reverse engineering

— 03 · Our Workflow

From oral spec or blueprint to an approved sample.

No finalised CAD file? Send a rough sketch, a worn sample, or an oral functional requirement. We generate the drawing, machine the sample, and only cut batch steel after your sign-off.

STAGE 01Intake

Drawing, sample, or oral spec

Send a formal 2D/3D print, a physical worn sample, or a verbal functional requirement to our Bengaluru toolroom.

STAGE 02CAD

Drawing generation

For oral or sample-driven jobs, we produce the full engineering drawing internally with tolerance and material callouts.

STAGE 03Sign-off

Customer confirmation

The drawing is submitted to your QA or R&D team for dimensional verification and formal sign-off.

STAGE 04Prototype

Physical sample fabrication

Upon confirmation, we machine, harden, and precision-grind initial sample parts for fitment testing at your facility.

STAGE 05Production

Batch consignment

Only after sample approval do we proceed to batch production — delivered in consignment sizes tailored to your schedule.

— 05 · Quality Assurance

Built to your print. Verified before dispatch.

Every part we produce is measured against the approved drawing before it leaves the toolroom. For metrology gauges, conformity is demonstrated through Go/No-Go functional verification plus dimensional inspection of the master feature.

Material certificates and calibration reports can be issued on request.

Certification status

ISO 9001:2015, AS9100, and NABL calibration traceability are [TO CONFIRM] pending client documentation.

QA-01

Drawing sign-off before metal is cut

No manufacturing order proceeds to production until the technical drawing has been formally signed off by the buyer's QA or R&D team.

QA-02

Sample approval before batch

Prototype parts are machined and shipped for physical fitment and metrology validation at the customer's facility. Batch production only begins after written sample approval.

QA-03

Hardness & material verification

Gauge-grade steel is heat-treated and hardness-tested. Where specified, hardness of 58–62 HRC is confirmed on a per-lot basis. Material test certificates can be issued with dispatch.

QA-04

Dimensional inspection report

Critical dimensions are measured against the drawing and recorded. A dimensional inspection report is supplied with the dispatch for metrology gauge orders. [REPORT TEMPLATE TO CONFIRM]

QA-05

Calibration traceability
